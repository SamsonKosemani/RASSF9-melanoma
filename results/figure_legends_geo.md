# Belote keratinocyte axis figure legends


**Figure. RASSF9 across the basal-to-differentiated axis of human keratinocytes.** (A) Percentage of keratinocytes with RASSF9 detected, by quintile of a differentiation axis defined as log2 of suprabasal (KRT1, KRT10, KRTDAP, DSP, involucrin, filaggrin, loricrin, TGM1, SPINK5, CASP14) over basal (KRT5, KRT14, COL17A1, ITGA6, ITGB1, TP63, KRT15) marker counts; annotations give detected over total cells. All cells were thinned to a common depth of 153,496 counts beforehand, and differentiating keratinocytes detect fewer genes overall, so any enrichment is obtained against that gradient. (B) Each keratinocyte placed on the differentiation axis against its basal marker counts, coloured by RASSF9 detection. Data from Belote et al., Nat Cell Biol 2021 (GSE151091), 22 donors. The prediction tested is from Lee et al., PLoS ONE 2011, which localised RASSF9 protein to the suprabasal epidermis in mouse.
